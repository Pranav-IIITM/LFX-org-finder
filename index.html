Contributor Guide & FAQ

Everything you need to know about the LFX program.

What is LFX Mentorship?
It's a platform by the Linux Foundation that connects open-source beginners with maintainers of CNCF and LF projects for paid, 3-month full-time internships.
How do I apply?
You apply directly on the LFX Mentorship platform. You'll need to submit a Statement of Purpose (Cover Letter) and a resume.
Are these mentorships paid?
Yes! LFX provides stipends for mentees who successfully complete their project milestones. The stipend amount depends on your country of residence.
When is the next term?
Check the Timeline card at the top of the page for the most current dates. The year is typically divided into Term 1 (Spring), Term 2 (Summer), and Term 3 (Fall).
